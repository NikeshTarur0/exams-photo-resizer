Comprehensive Guide to Converting PDF Documents to JPG & PNG Images

When applying for government jobs, educational admissions, or state recruitment drives, candidates are frequently instructed to upload supporting certificates (such as 10th/12th marksheets, graduation degrees, caste certificates, or category certificates) as single-page high-resolution JPG or PNG images rather than PDF files. Our free PDF to Image Converter renders PDF pages into high-definition image files locally in your browser.

Client-Side PDF Rendering Security

We utilize browser-based PDF parsing scripts to extract document vector objects into rasterized HTML5 Canvas elements. Because the conversion executes inside your web browser's isolated sandbox, sensitive marksheets, Aadhar cards, and private certificates are never sent to external servers or remote cloud APIs.
