How to Create a Passport Photo from Your Smartphone in 4 Steps

  1. Stand Against a Plain Light Wall: Stand 2 feet in front of a white or light-colored wall under good natural daylight. Avoid harsh shadows behind your ears or shoulders.
  2. Look Straight at the Camera: Maintain a neutral facial expression with eyes open and mouth closed. Ensure shoulders are level and facial features are centered.
  3. Upload to Passport Photo Maker: Drag and drop your image into our workspace. Select your standard preset (Indian Passport 3.5x4.5cm, US Visa 2x2", NEET Postcard 4x6", or SSC photo).
  4. Adjust Face Zoom & Download: Use our interactive face zoom slider to position face coverage precisely between 70% and 80%, then click Download Ready Passport Photo.

Key Passport Photo Rules to Avoid Rejection

  • Facial Coverage (70-80% Rule): The distance from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head must occupy roughly 70-80% of the photograph height.
  • Headgear & Accessories: Caps, sunglasses, Bluetooth headsets, or uniforms are not allowed. Head coverings for religious purposes are permitted provided the oval of the face from forehead to chin is fully clear.
  • No Red-Eye or Reflections: Flash reflections on eyeglasses lenses or red-eye effects will cause automatic rejection. Take off glasses if reflections appear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official size of an Indian Passport photo in pixels?

The standard size is 35mm x 45mm, which converts to 350x450 pixels when rendered at 300 DPI. The background color must be white, and the face must cover 70-80% of the photo height.

What are the photo dimensions for a US Visa online application?

A US Visa photo must be square (2 inches x 2 inches or 5.1 cm x 5.1 cm). In digital pixels, it is 600x600 px (minimum) to 1200x1200 px (maximum), with a target file size under 240 KB.

Why does the background color matter?

Automated facial recognition scanners used by passport offices and immigration portals require consistent light, solid backgrounds to accurately map facial features. Photos with busy backdrops are automatically rejected.
