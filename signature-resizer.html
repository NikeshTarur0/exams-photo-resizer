Comprehensive Guide to Online Exam Signature Preparation & Resizing

Digital signatures uploaded to competitive examination portals in India (such as SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NTA NEET, SBI, Railway RRB, and GATE) undergo automated optical verification. Over 10% of form rejections occur because signatures are blurry, signed on ruled paper, saved in PNG/WEBP format instead of JPEG, or exceed portal file size parameters (typically 10 KB to 20 KB).

How to Prepare a Compliant Digital Signature in 4 Easy Steps

  1. Sign on Unlined Paper: Use a crisp white unlined sheet (A4 paper or printer paper). Sign using a fresh black or dark blue ballpoint pen. Avoid gel pens that bleed into paper fibers.
  2. Capture with Phone Camera: Take a photo under good daylight or overhead LED lighting. Keep the camera flat directly above the paper to avoid angle distortion.
  3. Upload & Enhance: Drag and drop your image into our Signature Resizer. Enable the Enhance Signature Ink filter to automatically turn yellow lighting artifacts to pure white and darken the ink strokes.
  4. Select Preset & Download: Pick your target exam preset (SSC, IBPS, UPSC, or Custom) and download your 100% compliant JPG file.

Common Signature Rejection Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

  • BLOCK / CAPITAL LETTERS: Exam bodies strictly reject signatures signed in full CAPITAL letters (e.g., "N I K E S H"). Signatures must be written in continuous running script.
  • Lined Notebook Paper: Horizontal blue lines from notebook pages bleed into the signature scan, tricking optical verification scripts into rejecting the file.
  • Shadow & Camera Darkness: Phone shadows cast across the paper result in grey backgrounds instead of white. Our built-in Enhance filter removes paper shadows in real-time.
  • Wrong Extension (PNG/WEBP): Most portals reject `.png` files. Our tool automatically converts and encodes images to standard baseline `.jpg`.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard pixel size of an SSC signature?

For Staff Selection Commission (SSC) forms, signatures must be rescaled to 140x60 pixels (minimum) to 280x140 pixels. The file size limit is strictly 10 KB to 20 KB.

Should the signature be in JPG or PNG format?

Virtually all Indian exam portals (including UPSC, IBPS, SSC, and state recruitments) require signatures to be uploaded in JPG or JPEG format. PNG files with alpha channels are rejected.

How does the Enhance Signature Ink filter work?

It processes pixels directly on your local browser HTML5 Canvas. It converts yellowish shades typical of room-light phone photos into pure white while increasing black/blue pen stroke contrast.

Is my signature image uploaded to any server?

No. 100% of processing occurs client-side in your web browser. Your signature file is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database, guaranteeing complete privacy.

Can I sign with a blue pen or must it be black?

Both black and dark blue ballpoint pens are accepted by almost all portals including UPSC, SSC, and NEET. However, IBPS banking portals specify black ink strictly.
