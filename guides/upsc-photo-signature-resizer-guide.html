1. Overview of UPSC One Time Registration (OTR) Photo Requirements

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts prestigious examinations including the Civil Services Examination (CSE), Indian Forest Service (IFS), Combined Defence Services (CDS), National Defence Academy (NDA), and Engineering Services Examination (ESE). Every applicant must complete their One Time Registration (OTR) profile before submitting an exam application.

UPSC enforces strict digital scanning and aspect ratio parameters. Uploading an improper photograph or blurry signature results in automatic rejection of the OTR form or non-issuance of the e-Admit Card.

3. Detailed Guidelines for UPSC Candidate Photograph

  • Recency Rule: The photograph must not be older than 10 days from the opening date of the UPSC notification.
  • Name and Date Printing: The photograph must clearly mention the candidate's full name and the exact date on which the photo was taken printed at the bottom of the photo block.
  • Facial Visibility: The candidate's face must occupy 3/4th (approx 75%) of the frame. Both ears must be clearly visible.
  • No Accessories: Caps, dark sunglasses, goggles, masks, or headgear that obscures facial features are strictly prohibited (religious headwear is allowed provided the full oval of the face is visible).
  • Appearance Match: Candidates must maintain the same facial appearance (e.g. beard, glasses) throughout the Preliminary, Mains, and Interview stages.

4. Detailed Guidelines for UPSC Digital Signature Upload

  • Ink & Paper: Sign clearly on a piece of crisp white unlined paper using a fresh dark blue or black ink ballpoint pen.
  • Running Hand Only: Signatures in capital block letters (e.g., "N I K E S H") are considered invalid and will be rejected.
  • Lighting & Scanning: Avoid shadows or yellow camera light. Crop tightly around the signature stroke so background border margins do not push file size unnecessarily.
  • Self Signatures: The signature must be made solely by the applicant. If a proxy signature is detected during verification, candidacy is disqualified.
