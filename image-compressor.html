How Binary Search Compression Works

Standard graphic software requires users to manually guess JPEG quality percentages (e.g. 75%, 60%, 42%) repeatedly until the resulting file falls under a target KB limit. Our algorithm uses an intelligent binary search tree across the JPEG quality factor spectrum (0.0 to 1.0) on HTML5 Canvas. Within 5 milliseconds, it locates the exact quality index that maximizes image sharpness while guaranteeing the file size stays strictly inside your specified KB boundaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compress my signature to less than 20 KB?

Upload your signature scan, set the "Target Max File Size" field to 20 KB (or click the quick "20 KB" preset pill), and our engine will find the perfect quality scale to keep it under 20 KB instantly.

Will my image quality drop during compression?

We preserve pixel dimensions. Only high-frequency color noise invisible to human eyes is compressed. This guarantees that text strokes, facial contours, and signatures remain sharp and clear.

Is this compression tool completely free and private?

Yes. All operations occur in your local web browser using client-side HTML5 APIs. No pictures are transmitted over the internet or saved to remote databases.
