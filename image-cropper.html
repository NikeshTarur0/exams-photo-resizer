Comprehensive Guide to Image Cropping for Online Exam Forms

When preparing documents for online competitive exam applications, candidates often start with high-resolution smartphone selfies or full-page scans. Attempting to force full-frame photos into standard passport boxes leads to stretched faces or cut-off signatures. Using an accurate aspect ratio cropper isolates the essential facial box or signature stroke to fit rigid application guidelines.

What ratio should I use to crop signature scans?
