1. Official Staff Selection Commission Upload Rules

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts major national recruitments including SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level), SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level), SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff), SSC GD Constable, and SSC CPO. On the updated official portal (ssc.gov.in), candidates must fulfill precise digital upload guidelines.

3. Live Capture vs. Uploaded Photo Guidelines for SSC

On the new SSC portal (MySSC App / Portal web cam live photo capture), candidates are required to take a live photograph or upload a compliant portrait photo. Ensure:

  • Lighting: Face the light source directly. Avoid shadow patches across eyes or nose.
  • Background: Use a plain white or light grey backdrop. Wall patterns or textured curtains are rejected.
  • Spectacles & Cap: Do not wear caps, hats, or tinted/colored eyeglasses. Standard transparent reading glasses are allowed if eyes are clear without lens reflection.
  • Facial Expression: Keep a neutral expression with eyes fully open looking straight at the camera.
