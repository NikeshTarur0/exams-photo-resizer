2. Mandatory Requirements for NEET Photographs

  • 80% Facial Coverage: Face must take up at least 80% of the photograph area with both ears clearly visible without hair covering.
  • White Background: Background must be crisp, solid white without any shadows or colored wall backdrops.
  • Name and Date Tag: Candidate's name along with the date on which the photograph was taken must be clearly printed at the bottom of the photo.
  • No Spectacles/Polarized Lenses: Goggles or tinted glasses are strictly forbidden. Regular clear glasses are allowed if there is no camera reflection on lenses.
